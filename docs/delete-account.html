VisionCheck — Delete Account & Data

Last updated: September 2026
Short answer: there is no VisionCheck account to delete — the app has no sign-in and no server. Everything it records lives only on your own phone, and you're always in full control of erasing it, either from inside the app or by uninstalling it.

Why there's nothing to "delete" on our end

VisionCheck doesn't ask you to create an account, doesn't collect any personal information, and never sends anything it records to a server — there is no server. That means we hold no copy of your test history, your language preference, or anything else the app shows you. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail.

How to erase your data on your device

You have two ways to remove everything VisionCheck has stored locally:

Option 1 — Clear history from inside the app

  1. Open VisionCheck
  2. Go to the Settings tab
  3. Tap Clear all history (or تمام تاریخ مٹائیں in Urdu)
  4. Confirm when asked

This permanently deletes every saved self-assessment on your device. Your language and age-group preference are kept so the app still works the way you left it — those aren't sensitive data (see our Privacy Policy), but if you want those gone too, use Option 2.

Option 2 — Uninstall the app

Uninstalling VisionCheck from your device (long-press the app icon → Uninstall, or via Android Settings → Apps) removes the app and all of its locally stored data completely. Because allowBackup is disabled for this app, none of that data is included in Android's cloud backup either — uninstalling is a clean, complete removal.

If you shared or exported a result

If you ever downloaded a PDF report or shared a summary (e.g. via WhatsApp or email), that copy lives wherever you saved or sent it — your device's Downloads folder, or the app you shared it to. Deleting that is up to you in that destination app, the same as any other file you send from your phone; it was never something we had a copy of either.
