2. What VisionCheck is — and is not

VisionCheck is a self-screening tool that helps you notice possible changes in your vision using a set of standardised self-tests (distance vision, near vision, astigmatism, contrast sensitivity, an Amsler grid check, and colour vision).

VisionCheck is NOT a medical device and does NOT provide a medical diagnosis. It is a screening filter only. Results indicate whether it may be worth seeing a professional — they are never a substitute for a comprehensive eye examination by a qualified ophthalmologist or optometrist.

If you experience sudden vision loss, severe eye pain, or flashes of light, do not rely on the App — seek emergency medical attention immediately.
