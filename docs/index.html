Six standard vision tests

Guided by your symptoms, or run all six as a full check — each test is DPI-calibrated so what you see on screen is physically accurate to real clinical distances.

🔭

Distance Vision

Tumbling-E test for short-sightedness, tested eye by eye.

📖

Near Vision

Reading-distance text sizes, adjusted for presbyopia by age.

〰️

Astigmatism

A starburst fan chart checks for uneven corneal curvature.

🌫️

Contrast Sensitivity

Faded text at four levels — an early cataract indicator.

⊞

Amsler Grid

Central vision check for macular changes — flags urgently if abnormal.

🎨

Colour Vision

Simplified plate-style test for colour vision deficiency.

Built privacy-first

There is no backend. VisionCheck was designed so we never see your data — because we never receive it.

No account, no loginOpen the app and start — nothing to sign up for.
Nothing sent anywhereThe app makes zero network requests. Everything stays on your device.
You control your historyClear it any time from Settings, or uninstall to remove everything.
No ads, no analyticsNothing tracks you. The app is simply free to use.
VisionCheck is a self-screening tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose anything and does not replace a professional eye examination by a qualified ophthalmologist or optometrist. If you experience sudden vision loss, severe eye pain, or flashes of light, do not rely on this app — seek emergency medical attention immediately.
