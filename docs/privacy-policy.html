VisionCheck — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026
Summary: VisionCheck does not collect or send us any personal data, and your test results never leave your phone. There is no account, no login, and no server of ours. The app is free and shows banner ads from Google AdMob on its Home and History screens; AdMob may process a device advertising identifier to deliver them (details below). Your test answers and results are never shared with AdMob or anyone else.

Who we are

VisionCheck is developed by HomiLabs Solutions SMC Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the VisionCheck Android app handles information.

What the app does

VisionCheck is a self-screening tool that helps you notice potential changes in your vision before seeing an eye care professional. It is not a medical device and does not provide a medical diagnosis.

Data we collect

None. VisionCheck has no backend server of its own. We do not run analytics or crash-reporting services, and the app does not ask you to create an account or sign in. The only network activity is the loading of ads, described next.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

VisionCheck is free and supported by banner ads served by Google AdMob. Ads appear only on the Home and History screens — never during a vision test, on a result screen, or on the disclaimer.

  • What AdMob may process: your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate device and app information, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, in order to deliver, measure and secure ads. This is collected and used by Google, not by us.
  • What AdMob never receives from us: your test answers, results, history, age group or language. These stay on your device.
  • No personalised ads: the app requests non-personalised ads only, and limits ad content to a general/parental-guidance rating.
  • Your choices: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions, Google's consent form appears the first time ads load, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy options. On any phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Offline: with no internet connection no ad is requested, and every test, result and history feature still works.

See how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Data stored on your device

To show you your own test history, the app saves the following locally, using SQLite, only on your phone:

  • The language and age-band you selected
  • Dates and results of vision self-assessments you complete
  • Alert levels (green/yellow/red/urgent) generated from your answers

This information never leaves your device. It is not visible to us, to HomiLabs Solutions, to AdMob, or to any other party. You can permanently erase it at any time from Settings → Clear History, or by uninstalling the app.

Sharing and exporting results

If you choose to export or share a summary (for example, as a PDF or text message to show a doctor), that action uses your device's own share/print system feature. The file is created locally and is only sent to the destination you pick (e.g. WhatsApp, email, Save to device). We never receive a copy.

Permissions

VisionCheck does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage access. It uses standard Android network access and the Android advertising ID, both only so that Google AdMob can load and measure ads.

Children's privacy

VisionCheck is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app screens users under 13 out of the self-test flow and recommends seeing a specialist directly. Ads are limited to a general/parental-guidance content rating.

Doctor directory disclaimer

Where shown, listings of doctors, optometrists, or opticians are based on self-registration by those individuals. Their credentials and availability are not independently verified by us, and any contact you make with a listed practitioner is at your own discretion and risk.
