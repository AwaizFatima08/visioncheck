VisionCheck — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026
Summary: VisionCheck does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to any third party. There is no account, no login, and no server. Everything the app records stays only on your device, in a local database you control.

Who we are

VisionCheck is developed by HomiLabs Solutions SMC Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the VisionCheck Android app handles information.

What the app does

VisionCheck is a self-screening tool that helps you notice potential changes in your vision before seeing an eye care professional. It is not a medical device and does not provide a medical diagnosis.

Data we collect

None. VisionCheck has no backend server and makes no network requests. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services, and it does not ask you to create an account or sign in.

Data stored on your device

To show you your own test history, the app saves the following locally, using SQLite, only on your phone:

  • The language and age-band you selected
  • Dates and results of vision self-assessments you complete
  • Alert levels (green/yellow/red/urgent) generated from your answers

This information never leaves your device. It is not visible to us, to HomiLabs Solutions, or to any other party. You can permanently erase it at any time from Settings → Clear History, or by uninstalling the app.

Sharing and exporting results

If you choose to export or share a summary (for example, as a PDF or text message to show a doctor), that action uses your device's own share/print system feature. The file is created locally and is only sent to the destination you pick (e.g. WhatsApp, email, Save to device). We never receive a copy.

Permissions

VisionCheck does not request any special Android permissions (no camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage access beyond the app's own private folder, which the operating system grants automatically).

Children's privacy

VisionCheck is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all. The app screens users under 13 out of the self-test flow and recommends seeing a specialist directly.

Doctor directory disclaimer

Where shown, listings of doctors, optometrists, or opticians are based on self-registration by those individuals. Their credentials and availability are not independently verified by us, and any contact you make with a listed practitioner is at your own discretion and risk.
