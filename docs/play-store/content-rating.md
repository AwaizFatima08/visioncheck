# Play Console — Content Rating (IARC) questionnaire guide

Category: **Reference, News, or Education** or **Medical** (Play Console's
IARC questionnaire is the same regardless; pick "Medical" if offered as a
category, otherwise "Utility/Productivity").

Answer **No** to all of the following (the app has none of this content):
- Violence (any form, realistic or cartoon)
- Sexual content or nudity
- Profanity / crude humor
- Alcohol, tobacco, or drug references
- Gambling (simulated or real)
- User-generated content shared with other users
- Ability to communicate with or share location with other users
- Unrestricted internet access (app makes no network calls at all)
- Shares user location
- Allows purchase of digital goods (no in-app purchases exist)

Answer **Yes / relevant** to:
- "Does the app provide medical or health information" → Yes, mark as a
  health/fitness self-screening reference tool, and note in the free-text
  field: "Self-screening tool with an explicit in-app disclaimer that it is
  not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis."

Expected result: the questionnaire should return the lowest rating tier in
every region (e.g. **PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone / USK 0**), since there is no
objectionable content of any kind.

## Target audience & content
- Target age group: 13+ recommended, though the app itself gates under-13
  users at the Age Band screen and redirects them to "see a specialist"
  rather than running self-tests on them.
- Ads: **No ads** in the app.
- In-app purchases: **None.**
