# Release notes — v1.0.1 (versionCode 2)

First public release. Play Console's "What's new" field has roughly a
500-character limit — the English version below fits comfortably.

## English (primary listing)
```
Welcome to VisionCheck — a free, offline vision self-screening app.

• 6 standard vision tests: distance, near, astigmatism, contrast, Amsler
  grid, and colour vision
• Tell us your symptom and we'll recommend the right tests, or run a full
  eye check
• Clear results in plain language, with guidance on when to see a doctor
• Fully bilingual — English and Urdu, including spoken audio
• 100% offline — no account, no ads, nothing ever leaves your phone

Not a medical device. A screening tool to help you know when it's worth
seeing an eye doctor.
```
(438 characters)
